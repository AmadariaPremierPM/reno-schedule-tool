How this works

Templates are discovered automatically from the "Schedule Templates - AUM" and "Schedule Templates - Third Party" workspaces in Smartsheet, purely by reading each sheet's name — there's nothing to import or configure. Add a new template in Smartsheet with the right naming pattern (e.g. "Arch & ID - GR, MR") and it shows up here within about 30 seconds automatically.
